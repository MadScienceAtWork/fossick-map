About this map

Guide only

Mining licence, exploration licence and other land info is a guide only and may be out of date. Before fossicking or digging, check current rules, land status and any licence holder's rights with Resources Victoria and the land manager (e.g. Parks Victoria, DEECA).

Prospecting & fossicking rules (Resources Victoria)

How to use

Tap anywhere on the map to see the rock type, old mines, licences or fossils at that spot. Use the layers button to turn data on and off.
